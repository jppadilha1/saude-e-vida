# **App Name**: Saúde e Vida Management

## Core Features:

- Login Authentication: Secure login page with password 'coxinha123' (to be updated by the administrator) for accessing the management dashboard.
- Dashboard Overview: A dashboard providing a quick overview of active students and pending payments.
- Attendance Tracking: Manage student attendance by marking presence in training sessions.
- Student Management: General student management, including adding and editing students.
- Membership Status: Manage the status of student membership including management of outstanding payments.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Vibrant orange (#FF8C00) to reflect energy and vitality, echoing the brand 'Saúde e Vida'.
- Background color: Soft orange (#FFDAB9), very desaturated, for a calm yet inviting feel.
- Accent color: Yellow-orange (#FFA500) to draw attention to key interactive elements.
- Headline font: 'Poppins' sans-serif font for headlines, conveying a modern and clean feel.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' humanist sans-serif for body text, combining modernity and readability.
- Use clean and modern icons relevant to student management and fitness activities.
- Responsive layout to ensure optimal viewing experience across different devices.
